# Security

If you find a vulnerability in QueryBen, please do not open a public
issue. Email **emailvenkatm@gmail.com** with:

- A short description of the issue and its impact
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- Affected version (`About` menu shows the build)
- Your OS and, if relevant, the connection or auth mode involved

Expect a first response within 72 hours. We'll confirm the report, agree
on a disclosure timeline, and keep you in the loop on the fix.

If you'd like credit in the release notes for the fix, say so in your
report and we'll include your name (or handle) unless you prefer to stay
anonymous.
